## What the app is

A daily practice built around the Quran, Sunnah, and reflection. Reflections are written by an editorial team, not by scholars, and each one cites the classical sources it draws on. Quran translations and hadith are cited so you can verify them at the source.

## What the app is not

- Not a substitute for a qualified scholar, imam, counsellor, or medical professional. The app cannot see or respond to what you write. If you are in crisis or having thoughts of harming yourself, contact local emergency services or a crisis line; the app lists some under Profile → Get help now, but we cannot guarantee those numbers are current.
- Not a source of religious rulings (fatwa). Where sessions touch on practice, they follow broadly accepted Sunni positions and note the source.
- Prayer times are calculated on your device from your location using the calculation method you choose. Verify against your local masjid if precision matters.
