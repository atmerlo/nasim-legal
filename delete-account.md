# Delete your Nasim account

**Nasim** is published by Imperium Advisors, LLC.

You can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app, at any time. You do not need to contact us.

## Steps

1. Open **Nasim** on your device.
2. Tap **Profile** in the bottom bar.
3. Scroll to **Account** and tap **Delete account**.
4. Confirm. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

If you cannot sign in to reach that screen, email **support@nasimapp.co** from the address on the account and we will delete it for you within 30 days.

## What is deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes all of the following:

- Your account and sign-in credentials
- Your display name and profile photo
- Your journal entries, reflections and duas
- Your emotional check-ins and their history
- Your completed sessions and streak
- Your reminder time and prayer-time calculation settings

This happens immediately and is irreversible. Nothing is kept in a recoverable state and there is no additional retention period. Backups of our database are overwritten on a rolling 7-day cycle, after which no copy of your data remains.

## What is not deleted

- **Store subscriptions.** Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play or App Store subscription. Cancel that in your Google Play or App Store account settings.
- **Anonymous, non-identifying counts.** Aggregate totals such as "how many sessions were completed this week" contain no personal data and cannot be linked back to you.

## Before you delete

If you want a copy of your writing first, use **Profile → Export my data** in the app. It produces a complete file of everything above.
