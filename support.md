# Support

Email **[SUPPORT_EMAIL]** and a person will reply.

Bugs and feature requests can also go in the [issue tracker](https://github.com/atmerlo/nasim-legal/issues).

## Common questions

**Export or delete my data.** Both are inside the app: Profile → Export my data, and Profile → Delete account. Deletion is immediate and permanent.

**Cancel a subscription.** Subscriptions are billed by Apple or Google. Cancel from your App Store or Google Play account settings; deleting your account in the app does not cancel a store subscription.

**Prayer times look wrong.** Check the calculation method under Profile → Prayer, Qibla & Hijri date, and compare with your local masjid.

**A citation looks wrong.** Every ayah and hadith is cited to its source. If you find an error, email the session title and the citation; corrections ship quickly.

## If you are in crisis

The app is not a substitute for professional or emergency care. If you are having thoughts of harming yourself, contact your local emergency number or a crisis line. The app lists lines by country under Profile → Get help now, and [findahelpline.com](https://findahelpline.com) covers 130+ countries.
